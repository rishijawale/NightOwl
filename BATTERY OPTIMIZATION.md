## Battery Optimization 

- **Lightweight airframe:** Reduce unnecessary structural and payload weight to lower power consumption.

- **Efficient propulsion:** Select motor–propeller combinations that provide required thrust at lower power.

- **Large, low-RPM propellers:** Reduce rotor speed while maintaining thrust, potentially improving both **noise and energy efficiency**.

- **Optimize payload:** Use only necessary sensors/computing hardware to avoid carrying excess weight.

- **Efficient flight profile:** Prefer efficient cruising over unnecessary hovering and rapid acceleration.

- **AI at the edge:** Optimize the AI model and inference hardware to reduce onboard computing power.

- **Smart route planning:** Optimize waypoints and patrol routes to minimize unnecessary flight distance.

- **Dynamic mission planning:** Adjust patrol routes based on risk rather than continuously covering low-risk areas.

- **Battery monitoring:** Continuously monitor voltage, current, state of charge and estimated remaining flight time.

- **Safety reserve:** Keep sufficient battery for return-to-home/emergency landing instead of using the entire capacity.

- **Automatic return:** Trigger Return-to-Home when the battery reaches a predefined safety threshold.

- **Battery swapping:** Use pre-charged batteries at forward/mobile bases to minimize downtime.

- **Multiple UAVs:** Rotate aircraft instead of keeping one UAV continuously airborne.

- **Tethered UAVs:** Use ground-powered tethered drones for selected locations requiring persistent surveillance.

- **Forward charging stations:** Position charging/battery-swap facilities closer to remote patrol sectors.

- **Mission-specific optimization:** Optimize endurance for **useful surveillance time**, not simply maximum flight time.
